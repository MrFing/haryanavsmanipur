④ Key Findings
📚

Which state has higher literacy?

Manipur

Overall literacy: 76.94% vs Haryana's 75.55%. Female literacy in Manipur (70.26%) is significantly higher than Haryana (65.94%).

⚖️

Which has better gender equality?

Manipur

Gender gap of only 13.32% compared to Haryana's 18.12%. Northeastern culture supports women's education more strongly.

⑤ Explanation (Why is there a Literacy Gap? Which State is Better?)

Haryana has a much larger literacy gap (18.12%) between males and females compared to Manipur (13.32%). This is because Haryana has historically had a patriarchal social structure, where female education was not prioritised — girls were often married early, kept at home, or considered less important to educate. Access to schools in rural Haryana was also limited. Manipur, a northeastern state, has a more progressive and inclusive cultural tradition that values women's participation in society, resulting in better female literacy. Overall, Manipur is the better state — it leads in overall literacy, female literacy, and gender equality, making it a stronger example of inclusive education in India.
